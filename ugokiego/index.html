Frequently asked questions

What is Ugokiego?

Ugokiego is an online tool that turns a still image into a short video with cinematic camera motion. The name comes from the Japanese word 動き (ugoki), "motion".

How does the free trial work?

Pro starts with a 1-hour free trial. A card is required. If you cancel within 1 hour, you are not charged. If you do not cancel, we charge $99 when the trial ends for one year of Pro.

Does the subscription renew automatically?

Yes. Pro renews every year at $99 until you cancel. We email you a reminder 7 days before each renewal.

How do I cancel?

Use the "Manage subscription" link in your receipt email, or email support@ugokiego.net. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep Pro until the end of the year you paid for.

What is your refund policy?

30-Day Money-Back Guarantee: within 30 days of any charge we give a full refund on unused credits. No questions asked.

What will appear on my card statement?

Your card statement will show "UGOKIEGO.NET".

Are my images uploaded or stored?

No. Rendering happens in your browser, so your images never leave your device.

Can I use the videos commercially?

Videos made with Pro include a commercial-use license. You must own or have permission to use the image you upload.

Which formats are supported?

Upload JPG, PNG or WebP up to 10MB. Videos download as WebM in Chrome, Edge and Firefox, and as MP4 in Safari.
