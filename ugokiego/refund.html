30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

Full refund on unused credits. No questions asked.

This guarantee applies to every charge for Ugokiego Pro: the first annual charge after your free trial and every annual renewal.

1. How the guarantee works

  • You can ask for a refund within 30 days of the date you were charged.
  • If you have not used any of the credits included with that charge, we refund 100% of the amount you paid.
  • If you have used some credits, we refund the full value of your unused credits. A year of Pro includes 1,800 credits (150 per month × 12), so each credit is worth 1/1,800 of the annual price. For example, if you paid $99 and used 30 credits, we refund $99 × (1,770 ÷ 1,800) = $97.35.
  • We do not ask why you want a refund.

2. The free trial

You are not charged during the 1-hour free trial. If you cancel before the trial ends, no payment is taken, so there is nothing to refund.

3. How to request a refund

Email support@ugokiego.net from the email address you used at checkout, or include it in your message. Please write "Refund request" in the subject. You do not need to give a reason.

4. When you get your money

We process refund requests within 2 business days. The refund goes back to the card you paid with, in the same currency. Your bank usually shows it within 5–10 business days.

5. What happens to your subscription

When we refund a charge, the Pro period covered by that charge ends and your subscription is cancelled, so you will not be charged again.

6. After 30 days

After 30 days from a charge we do not normally give refunds, but you can cancel at any time to stop the next renewal (see our Cancellation Policy). This does not affect your statutory rights, for example if the Service is faulty.
