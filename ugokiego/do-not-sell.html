Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information

Last updated: 10 October 2026

個人情報の販売・共有の拒否：本ページは英語で記載されています。日本語でのご質問は support@ugokiego.net までお問い合わせください。

1. We do not sell or share your personal information

STRATA PINNACLE RESOURCE TRADING does not sell personal information and does not share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are defined in the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA) and similar US state laws. We have not done so in the past 12 months.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or third-party analytics on ugokiego.net.

2. Global Privacy Control

If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal, we treat it as a valid request to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information.

3. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we collect, use and disclose.
  • Get a copy of your personal information.
  • Correct or delete your personal information.
  • Opt out of any sale or sharing of your personal information.
  • Not be treated differently for using these rights.

4. How to make a request

Email support@ugokiego.net with the subject "Privacy request" and tell us which right you want to use. We will verify your request by confirming the email address linked to your subscription, and we respond within 45 days. You may use an authorised agent; we will ask for proof that the agent is authorised.

5. More information

See our Privacy Policy for full details of the data we collect and how we use it.
