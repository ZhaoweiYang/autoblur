Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how STRATA PINNACLE RESOURCE TRADING ("we", "us") handles personal data when you use ugokiego.net and the Ugokiego service. We are the data controller for this data under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Data we do not collect: your images

The images you choose and the videos you create are processed entirely in your web browser. They are not uploaded to or stored on our servers.

2. Data we collect

DataWhy we use itLegal basis
Email address and name you give at checkoutTo create and manage your subscription, send receipts and renewal reminders, and answer support requestsPerformance of a contract
Billing details (card type, last 4 digits, billing country, payment history)To take payments, issue refunds and keep tax records. Full card numbers are handled by Stripe, not by us.Performance of a contract; legal obligation

Technical data (IP address, browser type, pages requested) in server logsTo run, secure and troubleshoot the websiteLegitimate interests
Settings stored in your browser (language, number of free previews used)To remember your choices. Stored only on your device.Legitimate interests

3. Who we share data with

  • Stripe (payment processing). Stripe processes your card and billing data under its own privacy policy at stripe.com/privacy.
  • Our hosting and email providers, who process data only on our instructions.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. See Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information.

4. International transfers

Some providers, including Stripe, may process data outside the UK. When they do, we rely on UK adequacy regulations or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum to protect your data.

5. How long we keep data

  • Account and subscription data: while your subscription is active and for 2 years after it ends.
  • Payment and invoice records: 6 years, as required by UK tax law.
  • Support emails: up to 2 years after the conversation ends.
  • Server logs: up to 30 days.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to restrict or object to its use, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. To use these rights, email support@ugokiego.net. We reply within one month. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law, you also have the rights described in our Do Not Sell or Share page.

7. Cookies and local storage

See our Cookie Policy.

8. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

9. Security

We use HTTPS for all pages, limit access to personal data to people who need it, and use payment and hosting providers with recognised security certifications.

10. Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above.

11. Contact

Data protection questions: support@ugokiego.net.
