Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 10 October 2026

アクセシビリティ方針：本ページは英語で記載されています。日本語でのご質問は support@ugokiego.net までお問い合わせください。

We want everyone to be able to use Ugokiego, including people with disabilities.

1. Our standard

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

2. What we do

  • All controls can be used with a keyboard, and keyboard focus is clearly visible.
  • A "Skip to content" link lets keyboard and screen-reader users bypass the navigation.
  • Form fields have labels, and images and example videos have text alternatives.
  • Example videos stop animating if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Text and controls are designed with sufficient colour contrast against the dark background.
  • The site works at phone width and with browser zoom up to 200%.
  • The site is available in English and Japanese.

3. Known limitations

  • The video preview is visual by nature. The example videos have no speech or sound, so they do not need captions.
  • Drag-and-drop upload is not usable by every user; you can always click or press Enter on the upload area to open the file picker instead.

4. Feedback and help

If you find a barrier on our site or need information in a different format, email support@ugokiego.net. We reply within 5 business days.

5. Review

We last reviewed this site's accessibility on 10 October 2026.
