Cookie Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Cookieポリシー：本ページは英語で記載されています。日本語でのご質問は support@ugokiego.net までお問い合わせください。

This policy explains how ugokiego.net uses cookies and similar browser storage.

1. What we use

We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies, and we do not use third-party analytics. We use only browser storage that is needed for the site to work:

NameTypePurposeDuration
ugokiego.langLocal storageRemembers your language (English or Japanese)Until you clear it
ugokiego.creditsLocal storageCounts how many of your 3 free previews you have usedUntil you clear it

This data stays in your browser. It is not sent to us.

2. Payment pages

When you go to checkout, you use Stripe's secure payment page. Stripe sets its own strictly necessary cookies there to process payments and prevent fraud. See Stripe's cookie policy at stripe.com/cookie-settings.

3. Your choices

Because we use only strictly necessary storage, we do not show a cookie banner. You can delete or block cookies and local storage in your browser settings at any time. If you do, the site will forget your language choice and free preview count.

4. Contact

Questions: support@ugokiego.net.
