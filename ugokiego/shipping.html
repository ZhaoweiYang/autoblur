Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

配送・提供ポリシー：本ページは英語で記載されています。日本語でのご質問は support@ugokiego.net までお問い合わせください。

1. Digital service only

Ugokiego is a digital, online service. We do not sell or ship physical goods, so there are no shipping costs, shipping times or customs fees.

2. How the Service is delivered

  • Free previews are available immediately on ugokiego.net, without an account.
  • When you start a Pro subscription, Pro features are available immediately after checkout, and a receipt is sent to your email address.
  • Videos you create are rendered in your web browser and saved directly to your device when you click Download.

3. Availability

The Service is available worldwide through a modern web browser (current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari), except in countries where we are not allowed to provide it under UK sanctions law.

4. Problems with access

If Pro is not available to you after payment, or you did not receive your receipt within 1 hour, email support@ugokiego.net. We reply within 1 business day.
