Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

解約ポリシー：本ページは英語で記載されています。日本語でのご質問は support@ugokiego.net までお問い合わせください。

You can cancel Ugokiego Pro at any time. There are no cancellation fees.

1. How to cancel

  • Online: open the "Manage subscription" link in any receipt email from us. It takes you to Stripe's secure customer portal, where you can cancel in one click.
  • By email: write to support@ugokiego.net from the email address you used at checkout. We confirm cancellation by email within 1 business day; the cancellation takes effect from the time we received your email.

2. Cancelling during the 1-hour free trial

If you cancel before the trial ends, you will not be charged. Pro features stop when the trial ends.

3. Cancelling after you have been charged

  • Your subscription will not renew, and you will not be charged again.
  • You keep Pro until the end of the 12-month period you already paid for.
  • If you cancel within 30 days of a charge, you can also ask for a refund under our 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee.

4. Renewal reminders

We email you at least 7 days before each annual renewal, so you have time to cancel if you no longer want Pro.

5. If we cancel

We may cancel a subscription that breaks our Terms of Service. If we cancel without cause, we refund the unused part of your paid period.
