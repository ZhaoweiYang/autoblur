If you believe content on ugokiego.net infringes your copyright, send a notice to our designated agent at support@ugokiego.net with "Copyright notice" in the subject. Under the US Digital Millennium Copyright Act (17 U.S.C. § 512(c)(3)), your notice must include:

  2. Identification of the copyrighted work you claim is infringed.
  3. Identification of the material you claim is infringing, with enough detail (such as the URL) for us to locate it.
  4. Your name, postal address, telephone number and email address.
  5. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent or the law.
  6. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorised to act on the owner's behalf.

We also accept equivalent notices under UK law.

3. What we do

When we receive a valid notice, we remove or disable access to the material promptly and, where we can identify one, notify the person who provided it.

4. Counter-notice

If you believe material was removed by mistake, you can send a counter-notice to support@ugokiego.net including: your signature; identification of the removed material and where it appeared; a statement under penalty of perjury that you believe it was removed by mistake or misidentification; and your name, address, telephone number and consent to the jurisdiction of the courts for your address.
