Effective date: October 13, 2026 · Last updated: October 13, 2026

This page sets out the information that Japan’s Act on Specified Commercial Transactions requires for online sales of Blockgoer Pro, the annual plan sold on our Japanese site. This English page is a translation provided for convenience. The Japanese version of this page is the authoritative version and prevails if the two versions differ.

Sales URLhttps://blockgoer.net/ja/ (Japanese site). The English site is https://blockgoer.net/.
ProductBlockgoer Pro, an annual subscription to an online service that generates game assets with AI (cover art / thumbnails, game icons, textures, avatar shirts / clothing images, character renders, UI kits / UI layouts and sound effects), delivered digitally as PNG and WAV files. Each paid year includes 1,200 credits.
Selling priceJapanese site: ¥16,999 per year (consumption tax included, charged in JPY), billed annually.
English site: US$99 per year (USD); sales tax or VAT may apply depending on where you live and is shown at checkout before you confirm.
The language of the site decides the currency, and you are charged the amount and currency shown at checkout. The plan starts with a 1-hour free trial (10 trial credits), during which nothing is charged.
Charges other than the priceInternet connection and data charges needed to use the service are paid by the customer. There are no shipping fees and we charge no handling or other fees. Because the seller is located in the United States, some card issuers may add an overseas transaction or currency-conversion fee, even when you pay in yen; any such fee is set by your card issuer, not by us.
Payment methodsCredit and debit cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, JCB and Discover. Payments are processed by a third-party payment processor that is PCI DSS compliant. We never see or store your full card number.
Payment timingA payment card is registered when you start the 1-hour free trial. Nothing is charged during the trial. Exactly 1 hour after the trial starts, the first annual fee is charged automatically, unless you cancel before the hour ends. After that, the subscription renews automatically every 12 months on the same date at the then-current annual price, until you cancel. We email a reminder at least 7 days before each renewal charge. If the price changes, we tell you by email at least 30 days before the renewal it applies to; a price change never applies in the middle of a paid year.
Delivery timingThe service is available immediately when the trial starts, once your card is accepted. After the first annual charge, Pro access continues without interruption and 1,200 credits are added to your account. Generated assets usually appear in your workspace within about a minute (longer at busy times) and are downloaded there. See our Shipping & Delivery Policy.
Contract term and renewalEach paid year lasts 12 months, and the subscription renews automatically every 12 months until you cancel. Unused credits expire at the end of each paid year and do not roll over. One free trial per person and per payment card.
Returns, refunds and cancellationBecause the service is digital, it cannot be returned. Instead, we offer a 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee: Full refund on unused credits. No questions asked. Within 30 days after any annual charge (the first charge or a renewal), you can request a refund by email or from Account → Billing. Refund amount = annual fee × unused credits ÷ 1,200; if you have used no credits, you receive 100% of the fee (for example, 120 credits used leaves 1,080 unused, and the refund is US$89.10, or ¥15,299 on the Japanese site). Trial credits are not counted. Refunds go back to the original payment method; we process them within 5 business days, and banks usually show them within 5 to 10 business days. A refund ends the subscription and removes the remaining credits for that paid year. After 30 days from a charge, that charge is non-refundable except where the law requires otherwise, but you can still cancel the next renewal.
You can cancel at any time in Account → Billing → Cancel subscription or by emailing us from your account email. Cancellation takes effect immediately for future renewals, and we confirm it by email. If you cancel during the trial, you are never charged. If you cancel after a charge, no further charges are made and you keep Pro access and your remaining credits until the end of the current paid year. See our Refund Policy and Cancellation Policy.
Withdrawal of application or cancellation of contractThe statutory cooling-off period does not apply to online sales (mail-order sales) under the Act. Instead, you can cancel before the trial ends without any charge, and our own 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee, described above under “Returns, refunds and cancellation”, applies after every annual charge.
System requirementsThe latest version of Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Apple Safari or Mozilla Firefox, and an internet connection.
Name on card statementCharges appear on your card statement as “BLOCKGOER”.
