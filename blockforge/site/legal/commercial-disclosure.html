Disclosures under the Act on Specified Commercial Transactions

Email addresssupport@blockforge.vip (replies within 2 business days, in Japanese or English)
Sales URLhttps://blockforge.vip/ja/ (Japanese site). The English site is https://blockforge.vip/.
ProductBlockForge Pro, an annual subscription to an online service that generates game assets with AI (thumbnails, icons, textures, clothing images, character renders, UI layouts and sound effects), delivered digitally as PNG and WAV files. Each paid year includes 1,200 credits.
Selling priceJapanese site: ¥16,999 per year (consumption tax included, charged in JPY), billed annually.
English site: US$99 per year (USD); sales tax or VAT may apply depending on where you live and is shown at checkout before you confirm.
The language of the site decides the currency, and you are charged the amount and currency shown at checkout. The plan starts with a 1-hour free trial (10 trial credits), during which nothing is charged.
Charges other than the priceInternet connection and data charges needed to use the service are paid by the customer. There are no shipping or handling fees. Some card issuers may add an overseas transaction or currency-conversion fee because the seller is located in the United States; any such fee is set by your card issuer.
Payment methodsCredit and debit cards: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, JCB and Discover. Payments are processed by a third-party payment processor that is PCI DSS compliant. We never see or store your full card number.
Payment timingA payment card is registered when you start the 1-hour free trial. Nothing is charged during the trial. Exactly 1 hour after the trial starts, the first annual fee is charged automatically, unless you cancel before the hour ends. After that, the subscription renews automatically every 12 months on the same date at the then-current annual price, until you cancel. We email a reminder at least 7 days before each renewal charge, and at least 30 days’ notice before any price change applies to a renewal.
Delivery timingThe service is available immediately when the trial starts, once your card is accepted. After the first annual charge, Pro access continues without interruption and 1,200 credits are added to your account. Generated assets usually appear in your workspace within about a minute (longer at busy times) and are downloaded there. See our Shipping & Delivery Policy.
Contract term and renewal12 months per paid year, renewing automatically until cancelled. Unused credits expire at the end of each paid year and do not roll over. One free trial per person and per payment card.
Returns, refunds and cancellationBecause the service is digital, it cannot be returned. Instead, we offer a 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee: Full refund on unused credits. No questions asked. Within 30 days after any annual charge (the first charge or a renewal), you can request a refund by email or from Account → Billing. Refund amount = annual fee × unused credits ÷ 1,200; if you have used no credits, you receive 100% of the fee (for example, 120 credits used leaves 1,080 unused, and the refund is US$89.10, or ¥15,299 on the Japanese site). Refunds go back to the original payment method; we process them within 5 business days, and banks usually show them within 5 to 10 business days. A refund ends the subscription.
You can cancel at any time in Account → Billing → Cancel subscription or by email. If you cancel during the trial, you are never charged. If you cancel after a charge, no further charges are made and you keep Pro access and your remaining credits until the end of the current paid year. See our Refund Policy and Cancellation Policy.
Withdrawal of applicationThe statutory cooling-off period does not apply to online sales (mail-order sales) under the Act. Instead, you can cancel before the trial ends without any charge, and our 30-day money-back guarantee applies after every annual charge.
System requirementsThe latest version of Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Apple Safari or Mozilla Firefox, and an internet connection.
Name on card statementCharges appear on your card statement as “BLOCKFORGE”.
