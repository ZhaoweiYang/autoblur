Motion tracking

The blur follows the subject, frame by frame

AutoBlur's AI automatically tracks moving objects, faces and vehicles. You do not need to manually mask every frame — the motion-aware tracker keeps the blur locked onto the subject as it moves.

  • Multi-face tracking. Two people, ten people, or 100+ faces in a crowded scene are detected and followed simultaneously.
  • Keeps identity across cuts. Each tracked subject gets its own toggle, so you can blur one person and leave the rest untouched.
  • Handles turns and partial views. Profiles, angled shots and difficult lighting still register.
  • Manual top-up. Anything the detector misses, you box in yourself — and it gets tracked too.

FAQ

Questions people ask before uploading

Is AutoBlur really free?

Yes. AutoBlur offers genuine free blur processing with no hidden paywalls — you can upload videos and photos, apply professional blur effects and download clean files ready for publishing. Free covers videos under 200 MB and 10 minutes; Pro raises those limits for longer footage.

Do you upload or store my video?

No. AutoBlur leverages advanced client-side technology to process videos securely — files are processed locally in your browser and are never uploaded or stored permanently on external servers. When you close the tab, the working copy goes with it.

What can I blur?

Faces (auto-detected by AI), backgrounds, license plates, text, logos and any custom object you select. The AI tracks moving elements throughout the video, so the blur follows the subject instead of requiring keyframes.

Does it handle more than one face?

Yes. AutoBlur's AI detects and blurs multiple moving faces simultaneously — whether the shot has 2 people, 10 people or 100+ faces in a crowded scene. Each tracked face is listed separately, so you can blur some and leave others visible.

Which file formats are supported?

MP4, MOV, M4V, AVI and MKV for video, with preview and HD export. For images, upload JPG, JPEG, PNG or WebP. Exports come back in formats like MP4, MOV or WebM while maintaining HD quality.

Is there a watermark on the export?

No watermark on any tier. The blur itself is rendered into the pixels of the exported file, so it can't be removed or peeled off afterwards.

Do I need to install anything or create an account?

Neither. AutoBlur is a completely web-based tool: upload any video or image, pick a blur tool and apply pixelation or blur instantly inside your browser, with no apps, plugins or signup.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. AutoBlur is fully responsive and optimised for mobile browsers, so you can blur a video background or pixelate faces directly on an iPhone, iPad or Android device.

Can I control how strong the blur is?

Yes — adjust blur strength for anything from a subtle depth-of-field effect to a stronger bokeh-style background, and switch between gaussian blur, mosaic, pixelation and a solid block.

What if the AI misses something?

Draw a box around it yourself. Manual regions are tracked exactly like detected ones, so a missed plate or a house number stays covered as the camera moves.
