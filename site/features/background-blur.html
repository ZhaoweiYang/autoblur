FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a green screen?

No. Background blur is subject-aware, so you can get the effect without a green screen, a DSLR lens or a complex editing timeline.

Can I control how blurry the background is?

Yes. Adjust the blur strength for anything from a subtle depth-of-field effect to a stronger bokeh-style background.

Does it work on video I filmed weeks ago?

Yes — the tool is built for treating existing footage after it has been filmed.

Will my face stay sharp?

That is the point of subject-aware blur: the foreground subject is preserved while the background is softened.

Can I blur a photo background too?

Yes, with the image background blur tool — upload a JPG or PNG and the AI detects the subject automatically.
