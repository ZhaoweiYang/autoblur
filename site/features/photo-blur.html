What you can blur

Whole frame, or just the part that matters

A photo blur tool is usually one of two jobs: making something look intentional, or making something unreadable. This does both.

The background

Separate the subject and soften everything behind it for depth and focus.

Faces in the shot

Anonymise the people in a group photo before it goes anywhere public.

Text and numbers

Cover names, addresses, account numbers and plates that shouldn't be readable.

A region you draw

Box any part of the frame and cover it with blur, mosaic, pixelation or a solid block.

The whole image

Blur the entire photo for a thumbnail backdrop or a soft cover image.

Full-resolution output

Downloads come back at the resolution you uploaded, with no watermark.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need an account or an app?

No. AutoBlur is a free photo blur tool that runs in your browser — no Canva account, no Photoshop, no mobile app install.

Which image formats work?

Upload JPG, JPEG, PNG or WebP and apply AI-powered blur in seconds.

Can I blur only part of the photo?

Yes — blur the background, the detected faces, a text region or any box you draw, and leave the rest untouched.

Is the download watermarked or downscaled?

Neither. Files come back at the resolution you uploaded, without a watermark.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The tool is fully responsive and optimised for mobile browsers on iPhone, iPad and Android.
