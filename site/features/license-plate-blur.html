Capabilities

Purpose-built for plates

A general object detector treats a plate like any other rectangle. A plate detector knows what it is looking for.

Driving footage

Dashcam clips, ride-alongs and street-view style recordings, where plates enter and leave frame constantly.

Moving and parked

Vehicles are tracked as they cross the frame, so the blur travels with the car rather than sitting still.

Compliance-friendly

Anonymise identifiable vehicles before footage is published, shared with insurers or handed to a regulator.

Angled plates

Plates seen from the side, partially occluded or at distance are still picked up by the detector.

Solid cover option

Switch from blur to mosaic or a solid block when the characters must be impossible to reconstruct.

Stays on your machine

Fleet footage is processed in the browser and never uploaded or stored permanently on an external server.

FAQ

Common questions

Does it catch plates on moving cars?

Yes. Plates are tracked over time so the blur follows the vehicle instead of requiring manual keyframes in traditional software.

Is this enough for GDPR-style obligations?

Blurring identifiable plates is the standard mitigation before publishing footage. BGBlur helps creators and transport fleets stay compliant with privacy laws, though the legal assessment for your jurisdiction stays with you.

Can I blur faces and plates in the same pass?

Yes — both detectors can run on the same clip, and everything is rendered in one export.

What about a plate the detector misses?

Draw a box around it. Manual regions are tracked exactly like detected plates.

How long can my dashcam file be?

Free covers videos under 200 MB and 10 minutes; Pro raises the limit to 1 GB and longer durations.
