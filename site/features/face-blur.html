Capabilities

Built for faces that move

A face detector on its own drops out the moment someone turns their head. Tracking is what makes the export usable.

Multiple faces at once

Advanced AI detects and blurs multiple moving faces simultaneously — 2 people, 10 people or 100+ faces in crowded scenes.

Follows the subject

The AI tracks moving faces throughout the video, so the blur follows the person instead of requiring manual keyframes.

Blur, mosaic or solid

Choose the treatment and the strength — soft blur for a friendly look, mosaic or a solid block when it must be unrecoverable.

Pick who gets blurred

Every tracked face is listed separately, so you can anonymise the crowd and keep your presenter sharp.

Manual top-up

If a difficult angle is missed, draw a box over it — manual regions are tracked exactly like detected faces.

Nothing leaves the tab

Files are processed locally in your browser and never uploaded or stored permanently on external servers.

FAQ

Common questions

Does face blur work on a video with many people?

Yes. BGBlur's advanced AI automatically detects and blurs multiple moving faces in video simultaneously, whether you have 2 people, 10 people, or 100+ faces in crowded scenes.

Can the blur be removed from the export?

No. The blur is rendered into the pixels of the exported file rather than laid over it, so there is nothing to peel back.

Do I have to blur every face?

No. Each tracked face has its own toggle — blur the bystanders and leave your subject visible.

Is my video uploaded anywhere?

No. Files are processed locally in your browser and never uploaded or stored permanently on external servers.

What if a face is missed?

Draw a box over it. Manual regions are tracked the same way detected faces are, so a difficult angle stays covered as the shot moves.

Does it work on photos too?

Yes — the same detector runs on JPG, JPEG, PNG and other common image formats.
