How naming works

Say what should go, not where it is

Traditional software makes you find the object and follow it. Here you describe it once and the recogniser does the finding.

Plain-language names

Blur objects by naming them — “car”, “logo”, “person” or “background items” — and the AI works out what you meant.

Several at once

Add multiple object names separated by commas and proceed; every match is covered in the same pass.

Motion-aware tracking

You do not need to manually mask every frame — the motion-aware tracker keeps the blur locked onto the subject as it moves.

Any style

Cover matches with soft blur, mosaic, pixelation or a solid block, at the strength you choose.

Manual override

Anything the recogniser shouldn't have to guess, you box in by hand — and it gets tracked the same way.

Stays local

Recognition and rendering both run in your browser; the file is never uploaded or stored on an external server.

FAQ

Common questions

How do I blur multiple objects at once?

Upload your video, select the Blur Anything feature, add multiple object names separated by commas and proceed — the AI will automatically blur those objects.

What kinds of objects can I name?

Everyday things the recogniser knows: “car”, “logo”, “person”, “background items”, screens, signs, bags and similar. Natural language meets intelligent object recognition.

Do I have to mask each frame?

No. AutoBlur's AI automatically tracks moving objects, faces and vehicles, so the blur stays locked onto the subject as it moves.

What if it blurs the wrong thing?

Untick the match you don't want covered, or narrow the name. Manual boxes are always available as a fallback.

Does this work on images too?

Yes — object naming runs on still images as well as video.
