FAQ

Common questions

Is the free plan a trial?

No. Free is a permanent tier with no watermark and no hidden paywall. You only need Pro if your files exceed the free size or duration limits.

Do you charge per export?

No. Free exports are unlimited within the size and length limits, and Pro removes the practical ceiling on processing.

Do I need an account to use the free plan?

No signup is required to blur and download on the free plan.

Where is my footage processed on a paid plan?

The same place as on Free: locally in your browser. Paying raises limits; it does not change where your file goes.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes — Pro is month to month, and cancelling drops you back to the free tier rather than locking your files.
