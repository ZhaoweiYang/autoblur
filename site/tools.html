Styles

Four ways to cover a region

Adjust blur strength to achieve subtle depth-of-field effects or stronger bokeh-style backgrounds — or switch to a style that makes the region unrecoverable.

Gaussian blur

Soft and natural. Best for backgrounds and anything that should still read as part of the scene.

Mosaic

Chunky blocks. The familiar broadcast look for faces and plates, and hard to reverse.

Pixelate

Finer than mosaic, still destroys detail. Good when the shape should stay readable.

Solid block

Total cover. The safest option for documents, ID numbers and legal deliverables.

FAQ

Common questions

Can I use more than one tool on the same video?

Yes. Faces, plates, backgrounds and manual regions are all part of the same pass — tick whatever should be covered before you export, and it is rendered in one go.

Which formats can I upload?

Common browser-playable video formats including MP4, MOV and WebM, plus JPG, JPEG, PNG and other common image formats.

Is any tool paid-only?

No. Every blur tool is available on the free tier without a watermark; paid plans raise the file-size and duration limits.

Do I need to install a plugin?

No. BGBlur is completely web-based — upload a video or image, select a tool and apply pixelation or blur instantly inside your browser.
