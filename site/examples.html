FAQ

Common questions

Are these real exports?

They are illustrative scenes rendered to show what each tool does to a frame — the detection boxes, mosaic blocks and background softening match what the tool produces.

Will the blur ever slip off a moving subject?

Tracking is designed to follow the subject between detections. If a fast turn or heavy motion blur causes a gap, add a manual region over that stretch and it is covered.

Can I keep one person visible and blur everyone else?

Yes. Each detected face is listed separately with its own toggle, so you can blur the crowd and leave your subject sharp.
