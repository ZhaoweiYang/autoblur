Frequently asked questions

What is Ugokie?

Ugokie (from Japanese 動く絵, "moving picture") is an online tool that turns a still image into a short video with cinematic camera motion.

Is it free?

Yes. Every new user gets 3 free credits. Paid plans add more credits, 1080p export and remove the watermark.

Which image formats are supported?

JPG, PNG and WebP up to 10MB. For best results use an image at least 1024px wide.

Are my images uploaded or stored?

No. Rendering happens entirely in your browser, so your images never leave your device.

Can I use the videos commercially?

Videos created on Pro and Studio plans include a commercial license. Make sure you own the rights to the image you upload.

What format is the downloaded video?

Chrome, Edge and Firefox export WebM; Safari exports MP4. Both play on all major platforms and editors.
